Hostinger introductory price vs renewal price

The coupon only lowers the first term. After that, the plan renews at Hostinger’s regular price, which is several times the discounted rate. Budget for the renewal before you choose a plan.

Take the Premium cart tested here. The first 48 months cost ₹119.20 a month (₹5,721.60 upfront, before GST). The cart then says it “renews after 48 months at ₹449/mo for 12 months”, about 3.8 times the introductory rate.

A single 12-month renewal at that rate is ₹5,388 before GST. That’s almost as much as the entire four-year first term.

Some practical consequences:

  • A longer first term keeps the low rate longer but needs more money today. For a short-lived project, a shorter term can be the cheaper total.
  • Free extras end sooner than hosting. The free domain and mailboxes on Premium are for one year, not the whole 48 months.
  • The coupon won’t help at renewal. Hostinger’s partner page says the 20% doesn’t apply to renewals.

Hostinger coupon vs sale vs referral discount

Hostinger’s headline sale and the HARSHILBAROT coupon are separate discounts measured against different prices, so their percentages can’t be added together. Compare checkout totals instead.

List price
The crossed-out reference price (₹599/mo for Premium in India). Sale badges such as “75% off” are measured against it.
Sale price
The already-reduced price everyone sees (₹149/mo for Premium, 48 months). No code is needed.
Coupon code
A code typed at checkout. HARSHILBAROT took a further 20% off the sale price (₹149 → ₹119.20).
Referral discount
The same 20%, attached automatically when you arrive through the partner link.
Introductory price
Whatever you pay for the first billing term, after all discounts.
Renewal price
What the plan costs from the second term onwards (₹449/mo for Premium).

“75% off” plus “20% off” is not 95% off. ₹599 to ₹119.20 is about 80% off the list price. And the number that actually leaves your account is the cart total after tax.

Hostinger coupon not working?

First check whether a discount is already in the cart. If you came through the referral link, Hostinger hides the coupon field because the 20% referral discount is already applied, and that’s expected. Otherwise, work through the checks below.

  1. Look for an existing discount. A line such as “20% referral discount applied” or another code under “Coupon applied” means your order already has a coupon. Hostinger allows one per order.
  2. Check the spelling. It’s HARSHILBAROT: twelve letters, no spaces. Copying it avoids typos.
  3. Check it’s a new purchase. Hostinger says the 20% doesn’t apply to renewals or to products you already own.
  4. Check the billing period. On 15 October 2026 the code was rejected on a 1-month term (“Coupon is not applicable to the selected product(s)”) but accepted on 12-, 24- and 48-month terms.
  5. Check region and currency. Storefronts differ; a total in a different currency isn’t a fault with the code.
  6. Allow for change. Promotions can end or change after the date on this page. That’s a possibility, not a diagnosis of your cart.
  7. Ask Hostinger. If an eligible new plan still won’t accept it, Hostinger’s support can see your specific cart.
